PLACE EXIFTOOL.EXE HERE

1) Obtain ExifTool for Windows directly from https://exiftool.org/
2) If the downloaded file is named "exiftool(-k).exe", rename it to "exiftool.exe".
3) Place exiftool.exe in this tools/ folder.
4) Do not copy any new .FIR, autoexec.bin or Magic Lantern .mo files onto the camera card.
No executable is bundled with QVFilm's package; ExifTool comes from its official maintainer.
